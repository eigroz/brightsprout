CURRICULUM & TEACHING

Where the words come from

Quests 1–15 use the spelling patterns, example words and common exception words in the Department for Education’s Year 2 programme. Quests 16–30 introduce Year 3 patterns and selected Year 3/4 statutory words in a gentler Year 3 sequence.

DfE English Appendix 1 ↗National curriculum ↗Spellingframe Year 2 ↗Spellingframe Year 3/4 ↗

Teaching support follows a child-friendly sequence: hear the sound, map it to letters, build the word, then retrieve it again later. Spellingframe inspired the presentation; the pathway itself is mapped to DfE content.
